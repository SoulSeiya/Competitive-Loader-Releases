Competitive Loader includes a minimal, unmodified FFmpeg 7.1.5 executable
used only to convert the user's locally installed opening movie.

Project: https://ffmpeg.org/
Exact source archive: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.5.tar.xz
License: GNU Lesser General Public License version 2.1 or later

Build configuration:
--disable-everything --disable-doc --disable-debug --disable-network
--disable-autodetect --disable-x86asm --enable-small --enable-static
--disable-shared --enable-ffmpeg --enable-protocol=file
--enable-demuxer=mpegps --enable-demuxer=adx
--enable-decoder=mpeg2video --enable-decoder=adpcm_adx
--enable-parser=mpegvideo --enable-encoder=mpeg2video
--enable-muxer=mpeg2vob --enable-filter=buffer
--enable-filter=buffersink --enable-filter=format --enable-filter=null
--enable-swscale --enable-w32threads --extra-cflags=-Os
--extra-ldflags=-static
